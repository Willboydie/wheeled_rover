1 Who does what

Three devices. Only the ESP32 is on the internet. Only the Teensy drives the wheels. Only the CCU decides the pose.

ESP32

Network gateway, with a cellular modem. The only network stack.

  • Fetches RTK corrections and passes them on
  • Hosts the status page
  • Takes settings and other user commands, and passes them on
  • Later, firmware for every device arrives here first

Teensy 4.1

Realtime MCU on this build. The chip may change. The job does not.

  • Passes corrections to the GNSS module and reads the fix back
  • Sends every sensor reading to the CCU
  • Forwards the CCU pose and its uncertainty to the status page
  • Drives the wheels at the speeds navigation commands

CCU

The external computer. Its program is out of scope. This is the overview.

  • Takes every Teensy reading, plus the cameras on the robot
  • Outputs a 5DOF pose: east, north, heading, roll, pitch. No height. That may be reversed.
  • Reports an uncertainty taken directly from the fusion
  • Navigation turns that pose into wheel-speed commands

2 Where the RTK fix is computed

On the LG290P, the green box. The ESP32 and the Teensy only carry the corrections in and the fix out.

3 How the rover is connected

The CCU sits beside the Teensy, on its own link. The cameras go to the CCU. They do not pass through the Teensy.

4 What moves on each link

Two links, and they stay separate. Replacing the Teensy does not move these jobs. Wheel commands never pass through the ESP32.

ESP32 Teensy

Gateway link

  1. Corrections and user settings go down.
  2. The Teensy passes corrections to the LG290P. The module computes the fix.
  3. The CCU pose, its uncertainty, and GNSS health come up for the status page.
CCU Teensy

Driving link

  1. Every sensor reading goes up to the CCU.
  2. The pose and its uncertainty come back down.
  3. Wheel-speed commands from navigation come down.

5 What the status page shows

The ESP32 hosts the page. The pose on it is the CCU’s estimate. The page does not compute one.

6 This build, and the later product

The product updates every device through the ESP32. This build updates the ESP32 only.

Now

  • The ESP32 updates its own application over the internet.
  • The Teensy is updated on the bench.
  • The LG290P is updated by hand.

Later

  • Every device is updated through the ESP32’s internet connection.
  • The Teensy may be replaced. The replacement is an open choice.
  • The two links stay as they are.
